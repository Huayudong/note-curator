Reading notes on "Slow Productivity": the four locks on attention struck me the most.

The author, Timmonse, argues that the brain can be locked by four things at any moment: stress, constant multitasking, information overload, and lack of sleep. Citing a lab experiment, she notes that after an interruption people take about 23 minutes on average to return to deep focus.

I remain skeptical of one recommendation, though. The book suggests blocking two fully uninterrupted hours every day; that sounds right but rarely survives real collaboration rhythms. The more practical idea in the same chapter may be batching messages into fixed time slots.

Overall this is a book worth reading twice: first for the arguments, second for the sources behind each experiment.
